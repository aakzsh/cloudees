Privacy Policy | PeakStreak

PeakStreak app is a productivity tool developed by Zanie Company as a free application. It is intended for individual use and is provided without any cost.

This page outlines our approach to privacy concerning the use of the PeakStreak app.

PeakStreak does not collect any personal information or data from its users. All tasks and data entered into the app are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

By using PeakStreak, you acknowledge and agree that all data entered into the app, including tasks and related information, will remain solely on your device and will not be shared with any external parties.

PeakStreak does not utilize any third-party services that may collect user information.

We do not employ cookies or any tracking mechanisms within the PeakStreak app.

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes to the Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page.

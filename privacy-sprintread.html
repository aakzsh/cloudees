Privacy Policy

Effective from 15 February 2026

SprintRead is a speed-reading and productivity application developed by Zanie Company and is provided as a free application.

This page informs users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information while using the SprintRead app.

SprintRead does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users. All content, including pasted text, PDFs, ebooks, reading progress, bookmarks, and preferences, is processed and stored locally on the user’s device only.

Any text copied and pasted into SprintRead remains entirely on the user’s device and is never uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

SprintRead does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party services that collect user information.

We do not use cookies, tracking technologies, or background data collection mechanisms within the SprintRead app.

SprintRead integrates device local authentication (such as biometric authentication or device security lock) solely to protect access to the app. Authentication is handled entirely by the user’s device operating system. SprintRead does not access, store, or transmit any biometric data.

By using SprintRead, you agree that all data you interact with in the app remains private to your device and under your control.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at zaniecompany@gmail.com.
